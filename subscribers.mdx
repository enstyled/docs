---
title: 'Subscribers'
description: 'The list of all email subscribers'
api: 'GET /subscribers.json'
---

<Note>Requires admin authentication</Note>

Visitors subscribe through the form on the job board, so there's no endpoint to add subscribers. A subscriber with `email_confirmed: false` hasn't clicked the link in their confirmation email yet and doesn't receive the weekly email. Unconfirmed subscribers are deleted 30 days after their last confirmation email.

<ParamField query="page" type="integer" default="1">
  The page of results, newest subscribers first, 30 per page. A page past the last one responds with `404`.
</ParamField>

<ResponseExample>
```json Response
[
  {
    "id": 1,
    "email": "subscriber@test.com",
    "email_confirmed": true,
    "created_at": "2025-01-09T18:21:59+00:00",
    "updated_at": "2025-01-09T18:21:59+00:00"
  }
]
```
</ResponseExample>
