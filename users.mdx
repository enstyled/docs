---
title: 'Users'
description: 'The list of all registered users'
api: 'GET /users.json'
---

<Note>Requires admin authentication</Note>

<ParamField query="page" type="integer" default="1">
  The page of results, 30 per page. A page past the last one responds with `404`.
</ParamField>
